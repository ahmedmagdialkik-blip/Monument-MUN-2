The Vision

ABOUT MONUMENT MUN.

A youth-led Model United Nations conference that merges diplomatic tradition with contemporary elegance, born from a passion for meaningful international discourse, bringing together ambitious young minds to engage in high-calibre debate, forge coalitions and leave a lasting mark on tomorrow's world.

Whether delegates are stepping into a committee for the first time or returning as seasoned delegates, it is where diplomacy is not just practised but perfected.

Stop 01 · 07

GREAT WALL OF CHINA.

China

Centuries of fortified ramparts and watchtowers follow mountain ridges, marking sovereign boundaries through history.

SECURITY COUNCIL

Agenda: Disputed borders and the use of force along them.
Stop 02 · 07

PETRA.

Jordan

Rose-red sandstone canyons carved into monumental facades, commanding the vital crossroads of water and desert commerce.

ECONOMIC & SOCIAL COUNCIL (ECOSOC)

Agenda: Shared water and trade routes in arid regions.
Stop 03 · 07

THE PYRAMIDS OF GIZA.

Giza, Egypt

The oldest wonder on the list and the only ancient one still standing.

UNESCO

Agenda: Returning cultural heritage held in foreign museums.
Stop 04 · 07

EIFFEL TOWER.

Paris, France

A beacon of iron engineering overlooking the urban heart of Europe, where environmental accords define the century ahead.

UNEP

Agenda: Cutting emissions in the world's great cities.
Stop 05 · 07

CHICHÉN ITZÁ.

Mexico

The stepped pyramid of El Castillo rises amidst the dense jungle canopy, aligned to equinox serpents and sacred ancestral memory.

UN HUMAN RIGHTS COUNCIL

Agenda: Indigenous land rights and sacred sites.
Stop 06 · 07

COLOSSEUM.

Rome, Italy

Arched travertine tiers where the ancient arena commanded combat, now confronting the modern ethics of warfare and autonomous machinery.

DISEC

Agenda: Autonomous weapons and human control.
Stop 07 · 07

TAJ MAHAL.

Agra, India

An ivory-white marble monument to devotion, standing serene under a pale night sky as delegates champion universal equality.

UN WOMEN

Agenda: Access to education for girls.
